    Roadmap to the platinum

    Four stages, in order, built from the official trophy descriptions. Story first (12 trophies), then the stranger strands (7), then activities and robberies (22), then the clean-up (14) that ends in 100% completion and the platinum.

    Missable trophies — the one-shot moments

    Nothing on this list is confirmed permanently missable from its official description: story missions can be replayed, and counters keep counting after the story. What is easy to miss is a one-shot moment — a trophy tied to a single mission or a specific setup. Do these deliberately, in the mission or setup named, and replay the mission if the game offers mission replay and the trophy does not pop.

      How this guide works

      • Saved progress: ticking a trophy saves it in your browser (localStorage). Export it to a file and import it back any time.
      • Filters: filter by kind (story, stranger strands, collectibles, activities, robberies, progress & money, platinum — or online, which the official list has none of), by grade, by missable-only, or search by name, mission or keyword.
      • Spoiler-safe mode: hides the names and descriptions of story trophies you have not earned yet.
      • Offline: this guide is plain static files. It loads nothing from the network and works with the network off.
      • No Rockstar artwork: the look is our own — no logo, box art or game screenshots.
